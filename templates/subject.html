Subjects we offer

In Studylog, we offer students with material to learn about for 9 subjects. For core subjects, we provide knowledge on Mathematics, English, Science, Geography and History. As for the elective subjects, Studylog has gathered materials on Coding, Japanese, Chinese and Commerce. Down below are our description on each subjects we offer.

Mathematics

This website provides students with materials on a range of different math topics. For stage 3, we offer worksheets on topics such as geometry and division. For stage 4, some topics that we provide are linear relationships and introduction to trigonometry. Studylog also provides further graph related information and questions for stage 5 students, and for stage 6, they get advanced trigonometry and past HSC paper. With these materials provided, it could support the boost, and allow you students to accel in mathematics.

English

Studylog's English resources supports students from year 5 to 12 with progression in text levels. In year 5, students build comprehension by reading "After the Flood", while in year 6, they strengthens grammar and figurative skills. Year 7 introduces Shakespeare, year 8 with Tempe High School practice exam and year 9 with the story "The Monkey's Paw". Year 10 focusses on "Romeo and Juliet" with a dedicated workbook and year 11 learners access essay samples. Lastly, year 12 students obtains Advanced English HSC 2024 paper. Together, these resources will guide students step by step toward stronger analytical skills, confident writing, and higher level exam performance.

Science

Our Science resources take students from core basics to senior exam preparation. year 5 begins with biology of Glass Animals, year 6 covering the Periodic Table, and year 7 builds circuit knowledge. Year 8 explores ecosystems, with year 9 extending practices on circuit drawing, and year 10 uses a full 2012 Chemistry past paper. Senior students strengthen content with year 11 biology revision and year 12 with physics HSC 2025 materials.

Geography

Studylog's Geography pathway develops strong spatial and global understanding. Year 5 starts with basic skills, year 6 examines on difference Australia and Asia, and year 7 studies floods. Year 8 focuses on map skills, year 9 revises the water cycle, and year 10 prepares for the Australian Geography Competition. Year 11 explores Asian Geography, while year 12 completes HSC 2025 preparation.

History

Our History resources builds chronological understanding and analytical skills. Year 5 covers Aboriginal and European contact, year 6 studies on the Australia's Parliament House, and year 7 learns about Ancient China. Year 8 investigates the Black Death, year 9 reviews Earth's history, and year 10 works through the Australian History Competition 2023. Senior students use a year 11 exam sample and year 12 Modern History HSC 2025 materials.

Coding

Studylog's coding resources helps build digital thinking for students from year 7 to year 12. Students begin with learning about PC component terminology and core computer science vocabulary, then move into the HSC Software Engineering website, which leads to CipherForge and game design theory in years 9 and 10. Senior students then focuses on algorithm design in year 11 and full HSC examination preparation in year 12.

Japanese

Our Japanese pathway supports steady language growth and exam readiness. First starting off in year with family vocabulary, year 8 then revises with Obento textbook, and year 9 uses N5 sample questions. Year 10 students explores on the topic "school life" through a Western Australia task. In year 11, our recommended resources is the N4 sample questions, leading to year 12's preparation with the 2025 ATAR Japanese Second Language examination.

Chinese

Chinese resources we offer develops core language skills and HSC readiness. Year 7 begins with a family themed practice assessment, year 8 with describing appearance, and year 9 consolidates pinyin understanding. Year 10 proceeds to focus on vocabulary, while year 11 uses 2024 HSC Beginners exam and transcript papers. Year 12 then progresses to 2025 HSC Continuers exam and transcript papers for further preparation.

Commerce

Studylog's commerce materials mainly builds economic and business understandings. Starting from year 7, we offer an economy quiz. Year 8 then extends into business and economic concepts, and year 9 uses a dedicated textbook. In year 10, we provide materials that focuses on yearly exam revision, and year 11 applies that knowledge through a business review worksheet. Lastly, year 12 can prepare with the 2025 HSC Business Studies examination.

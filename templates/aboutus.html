About Us

Who We Are

Studylog is a free-to-use online learning platform built to support students from Stage 3 to Stage 6 across a range of core and elective subjects. Our mission is to enhance the quality of studying for students who are keen to proceed in their learning journey. We provide notes, textbook , and online resources tailored to each year group. Whether you're preparing for an exam or just keeping up with classwork, Studylog is here to help you learn and achieve more.

Studylog's History

Studylog is designed and created in 2026, by Justin W, a year 9 computer technology student of Tempe High School. The website's creation follows the Year 9 Introduction of Software Engineering's lessons of Planning Your Very First Website, Python Flask & Bootstrap, and Documenting Your Website.

Our aim

Studylog aims to resolve the time consuming struggles of students when searching for relevant study materials online. Passionate learners are motivated and eager to improve, yet they often lose valuable study time sorting through countless websites, videos, and resources just to find information that isn't accurate or relevant to their learning. Our goal is to remove that barrier entirely. By providing high quality, and curriculum aligned notes into one website, Studylog ensures students can access everything they need without endlessly searching. Every resource is carefully selected, summarised, and suited to each year group we offer. Studylog exists to help students study smarter by making the right content easier to find, easier to understand, and all in one place.
